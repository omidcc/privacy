Puzzle Mama for iOS – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy covers the Puzzle Mama app for iPhone and iPad (bundle ID com.omi.puzzlemama), a daily puzzle app with number, word and memory games, published by tunerapps ("we"). The Android app has its own policy at tunerapps.net/privacy/puzzle-mama.

What we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information ourselves. Puzzle Mama has no accounts and no registration of its own. It never asks for your name, email address, location, photos, contacts or any other personal details, and we run no servers. The data that leaves your device is what the advertising service receives when an ad is shown, the anonymous usage and crash data described under "Analytics and crash reports" (which you can turn off in Settings), and, only if you choose to join the daily leaderboard, your puzzle times sent to Apple's Game Center.

What stays on your device

To remember your game, the app saves data only on your own device: today's puzzles and your progress in them, your results and best times, your streak and Full set badges, and your settings (sound effects, haptics, timer, mistakes, reduced motion, daily reminder). We never receive this data. You can erase it at any time with "Reset all progress" in Settings, or by deleting the app. It may be included in your iCloud or device backup, which Apple manages and we cannot access.

Advertising

Puzzle Mama shows ads from Google AdMob. An ad appears only when you choose to watch one for a bonus hint (a rewarded ad), or occasionally between puzzles (a full-screen ad). Ads never appear while you are playing a puzzle, and you never need to watch an ad to finish a puzzle, keep your streak or earn a badge.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google may receive and process information from your device, such as your IP address, device and operating system details, and how you interact with ads, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. If you allow tracking (see below), this can include the advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not receive this information. How Google handles it is described in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy. You can view or reset your ad personalisation choices in Google Ads Settings.

Consent and App Tracking Transparency

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before requesting ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. The app also asks permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not used and ads are not personalised. You can change this later in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security > Tracking. You can still play every puzzle whatever you choose.

Analytics and crash reports

Puzzle Mama uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google, to find crashes and to learn which puzzles are too hard or too easy. They are on by default and you can turn them off at any time with "Share anonymous usage and crash data" in Settings > Ads & privacy. They are always off in development builds.

When on, the app sends: crash reports (the error, app version, iOS version and device model); and a few simple events: onboarding finished, a puzzle started or completed, a hint used (free or from an ad), a rewarded ad result, and Full set earned. Each event can include the game type, difficulty, a coarse time range (for example "under 1 minute"), and counts of mistakes and hints. We never send your answers, names, email, location or contacts.

The data is linked to a random app-instance identifier created by Firebase, not to you as a person, and we cannot tell who you are from it. If you allow tracking, Google may also use the advertising identifier for ad measurement. Where consent is required (EEA, UK and Switzerland), Firebase follows the choice you make in the consent message. Google processes this data on our behalf; see Firebase privacy and security. We do not build profiles of you.

Daily leaderboard (Game Center, optional)

The daily leaderboard is off until you choose it. It starts only when you tap "Join with Game Center" or switch on "Show me on the daily leaderboard" in Settings. Before that, nothing is sent and you are not signed in to anything.

When you join, the app signs you in to Apple's Game Center and sends the time you took to finish each daily puzzle (and the total for a full set) to Game Center, so that you can be ranked against other players. Practice puzzles are never sent. The leaderboard is run by Apple, not by us:

  • Other players who view the leaderboard can see your Game Center name (the alias or nickname in your Game Center profile) and your time. We never see your Apple Account details.
  • To rank you with players who play on the same calendar day as you, the app sends your times to a board for your device's time zone, so Apple can tell your approximate time zone from the board used.
  • You can switch the leaderboard off at any time in Settings. The app then stops sending times and deletes any that were still waiting to be sent. Times already sent are held by Apple under its own rules; see Apple's privacy policy and Game Center and privacy. You can change your Game Center profile and who can see it in the iOS Settings app.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): asked for only if you turn on the daily reminder. The reminder is scheduled on your device and sends nothing anywhere.
  • Tracking (optional): the App Tracking Transparency prompt described above.
  • Game Center (optional): used only if you join the daily leaderboard.
  • Haptics: for game feedback, only if you turn them on in Settings.

The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and we collect no personal information. Google processes the information described under "Advertising" and "Analytics and crash reports", as ad provider and as our analytics processor, and Apple operates Game Center if you join the leaderboard.

Children

Puzzle Mama is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
