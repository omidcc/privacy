Puzzle Mama – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy covers the Android app Puzzle Mama (package com.omi.puzzlemama), a daily puzzle app with number, word and memory games, published by tunerapps ("we").

What we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information ourselves. Puzzle Mama has no accounts, no sign-in and no registration. It never asks for your name, email address, location, photos, contacts or any other personal details, and we run no servers of our own. The only data that leaves your device is what the advertising service receives when an ad is shown, and the anonymous usage and crash data described under "Analytics and crash reports", which you can turn off in Settings.

What stays on your device

To remember your game, the app saves data only on your own device: today's puzzles and your progress in them, your results and best times, your streak and Full set badges, and your settings (sound effects, vibration, timer, mistakes, reduced motion, daily reminder). We never receive this data. You can erase it at any time with "Reset all progress" in Settings, or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app in Android settings.

Advertising

Puzzle Mama shows ads from Google AdMob. An ad appears only when you choose to watch one for a bonus hint (a rewarded ad), or occasionally between puzzles (a full-screen ad). Ads never appear while you are playing a puzzle, and you never need to watch an ad to finish a puzzle, keep your streak or earn a badge.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google may receive and process information from your device, such as your IP address, device and operating system details, the Android advertising ID, and how you interact with ads, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. We do not receive this information. How Google handles it is described in Google's own policies: how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Where you choose a consent option for personalised ads, it applies to the ads in this app.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before requesting ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. If you do not consent, you can still play every puzzle.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only by the ad service and to know whether an ad hint can be offered. All puzzles work without a connection.
  • Notifications (optional): asked for only if you turn on the daily reminder. The reminder is scheduled on your device and sends nothing anywhere.
  • Advertising ID: declared because the Google Mobile Ads SDK can read it to show and measure ads.

The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Analytics and crash reports

Puzzle Mama uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google, to find crashes and to learn which puzzles are too hard or too easy. They are on by default and you can turn them off at any time with "Share anonymous usage and crash data" in Settings > Ads & privacy. They are always off in development builds.

When on, the app sends: crash reports (the error, app version, Android version and device model); and a few simple events: onboarding finished, a puzzle started or completed, a hint used (free or from an ad), a rewarded ad result, and Full set earned. Each event can include the game type, difficulty, a coarse time range (for example "under 1 minute"), and counts of mistakes and hints. We never send your answers, names, email, location or contacts.

The data is linked to a random app-instance identifier created by Firebase, not to you as a person, and we cannot tell who you are from it. We do not use the Android advertising ID for analytics and have switched off ad personalisation signals in Firebase. Where consent is required (EEA, UK and Switzerland), Firebase follows the choice you make in the consent message. Google processes this data on our behalf; see Firebase privacy and security. We do not build profiles of you, and Puzzle Mama has no social features or user-generated content.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and we collect no personal information. Google processes the information described under "Advertising" and "Analytics and crash reports", as ad provider and as our analytics processor.

Children

Puzzle Mama is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes
