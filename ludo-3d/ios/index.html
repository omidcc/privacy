Ludo 3D for iOS – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy covers the Ludo 3D app for iPhone and iPad (bundle ID com.omi.ludo3d), a board game app with Classic Ludo and Snake Ludo (snakes and ladders), published by tunerapps ("we"). The Android app has its own policy at tunerapps.net/privacy/ludo-3d.

What we collect

Ludo 3D has no accounts and no sign-in. It never asks for your email address, phone number, location, photos or contacts. The only server we run is the one for online games, described below. Apart from that, the data that leaves your device is what the advertising service receives when an ad is shown, the anonymous usage data described under "Analytics", and purchase confirmations from Apple.

What stays on your device

The app saves your settings (language, game mode, board theme, difficulty, rule options, sound, vibration and reminder), the player names you type, your coins, unlocked themes, daily-reward streak, and an unfinished game for each mode, only on your device. We never receive this data. Deleting the app erases it. It may be included in your iCloud or device backup, which Apple manages and we cannot access.

Online games

Playing online is optional. Offline games against the computer or friends on one device never connect to our server. When you start a quick match or a private room, the app connects to our game server (hosted on Cloudflare) and sends:

  • a random player ID that the app creates on your device the first time you play online (it is not linked to you, your Apple Account or your device identifiers);
  • the display name you type for online games, which the other players in your room can see;
  • the room code, and your game moves (rolls and token choices).

The server keeps this only while the game is running. An unused room is deleted after about 30 minutes, and a finished room about 10 minutes after the game ends. Like any internet service, our server and Cloudflare, which provides the network, can see your IP address while you are connected. We do not store it, and we do not use it to identify you. There is no chat, voice or any other way to send messages or files to other players. Please do not use your real name or personal details as your display name.

Advertising

Ludo 3D shows ads from Google AdMob:

  • Banners on the Home, Settings and Results screens. Never during a game.
  • Full-screen ads occasionally when you leave the Results screen after a game. Never during a game, and not on your first day of play.
  • Rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one, for example for free coins, to double a reward, for a Snake Shield or Second chance, or to try a board theme.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google may receive and process information from your device, such as your IP address, device and operating system details, and how you interact with ads, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. If you allow tracking (see below), this can include the advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not receive this information. How Google handles it is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can view or reset your ad personalisation choices in Google Ads Settings. Buying "Remove ads" removes banners and full-screen ads; rewarded ads stay available because you only see them when you choose to.

Consent and App Tracking Transparency

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before requesting ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. The app also asks permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not used and ads are not personalised. You can still play every game whatever you choose.

Analytics

Ludo 3D uses Google Firebase Analytics to learn how the games are played. The app sends simple game events such as a game started or finished (mode, number of players, computer or friends, difficulty, board theme, language), dice rolls, captures, tokens reaching home, snake bites and ladder climbs, which mode you picked, theme and language changes, rewarded ads watched and purchases. We never send the player names you type, your email, location or contacts.

The data is linked to a random app-instance identifier created by Firebase, not to you as a person, and we cannot tell who you are from it. Google processes this data on our behalf; see Firebase privacy and security.

In-app purchases

Purchases such as "Remove ads", the theme pack and the Premium bundle are handled by Apple. We receive a confirmation of what you bought so the app can unlock it, but never your payment details. You can restore purchases in Settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): asked for only if you turn on the daily reminder. The reminder is scheduled on your device and sends nothing anywhere.
  • Tracking (optional): the App Tracking Transparency prompt described above.
  • Haptics: for game feedback, only if you turn vibration on in Settings.

The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Google processes the information described under "Advertising" and "Analytics", Apple handles purchases, and Cloudflare carries the network traffic for online games.

Children

Ludo 3D is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
