Ludo 3D – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy covers the Android app Ludo 3D (package com.omi.ludo3d), a board game app with Classic Ludo and Snake Ludo (snakes and ladders), published by tunerapps ("we").

What we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information ourselves. Ludo 3D has no accounts, no sign-in and no online play. It never asks for your email address, location, photos, contacts or any other personal details, and we run no servers of our own. The only data that leaves your device is what the advertising service receives when an ad is shown, the anonymous usage and crash data described under "Analytics and crash reports", and purchase confirmations from Google Play.

What stays on your device

To remember your game, the app saves data only on your own device: your settings (language, game mode, board theme, difficulty, rule options, sound, vibration and reminder), the player names you type, your coins, unlocked themes, daily-reward streak, and an unfinished game for each mode. We never receive this data. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app in Android settings.

Advertising

Ludo 3D shows ads from Google AdMob:

  • Banners on the Home, Settings and Results screens. Never during a game.
  • Full-screen ads occasionally when you leave the Results screen after a game. Never during a game, and not on your first day of play.
  • Rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one, for example for free coins, to double a reward, for a Snake Shield or Second chance, to try a board theme, or for 30 minutes without full-screen ads.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google may receive and process information from your device, such as your IP address, device and operating system details, the Android advertising ID, and how you interact with ads, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. We do not receive this information. How Google handles it is described in Google's own policies: how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Buying "Remove ads" removes banners and full-screen ads; rewarded ads stay available because you only see them when you choose to.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before requesting ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. If you do not consent, you can still play every game.

In-app purchases

Purchases such as "Remove ads", the theme pack and the Premium bundle are handled by Google Play. We receive a confirmation of what you bought so the app can unlock it, but never your payment details.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only by the ad service, analytics, crash reports and Google Play purchases. Every game works without a connection.
  • Notifications (optional): asked for only if you turn on the daily reminder. The reminder is scheduled on your device and sends nothing anywhere.
  • Vibration: for game feedback, only if you turn vibration on in Settings.
  • Google Play billing: to make in-app purchases.
  • Advertising ID: declared because the Google Mobile Ads SDK can read it to show and measure ads.

The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Analytics and crash reports

Ludo 3D uses Google Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config, provided by Google, to find crashes, to learn how the games are played, and to tune things like how often ads appear without releasing an update.

The app sends: crash reports (the error, app version, Android version and device model); and simple game events such as a game started or finished (mode, number of players, computer or friends, difficulty, board theme, language and rule options), dice rolls, captures, tokens reaching home, snake bites and ladder climbs, which mode you picked, theme and language changes, ad views and purchases. We never send the player names you type, your email, location or contacts.

The data is linked to a random app-instance identifier created by Firebase, not to you as a person, and we cannot tell who you are from it. Where consent is required (EEA, UK and Switzerland), Firebase follows the choice you make in the consent message. Google processes this data on our behalf; see Firebase privacy and security. We do not build profiles of you, and Ludo 3D has no social features, chat or user-generated content.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and we collect no personal information. Google processes the information described under "Advertising", "In-app purchases" and "Analytics and crash reports", as ad provider, payment provider and our analytics processor.

Children

Ludo 3D is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
