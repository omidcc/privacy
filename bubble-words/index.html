Bubble Words – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy covers the Android app Bubble Words (package com.omi.bubblewords), a vocabulary game for children aged 3 to 6, published by tunerapps ("we").

What we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information. Bubble Words has no accounts, no sign-in and no registration. It never asks for a name, age, email address, location, photos, contacts or any other personal details. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send information anywhere, and it runs no servers of our own.

What stays on your device

To remember how far your child has got, the app saves a small amount of game data only on your own device: the stars earned, which levels are finished, and your settings (sound effects, music, voice, parental lock). This data never leaves the device. You can erase it at any time with "Reset progress" in the Parent area, or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app in Android settings.

Voice

The app reads letters and words aloud using the text-to-speech voice built into your Android device. That voice is part of Android, not of the app. The app does not record sound and does not use the microphone.

Advertising and tracking

Bubble Words contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting tools. It has no social features, chat or user-generated content, and it does not link to other websites or apps.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions. It only checks whether a text-to-speech service is available on your device.

Parent area

Settings are behind a "grown-ups only" question so that children do not change them by accident. The question is answered on the device and nothing is sent anywhere.

Purchases

Version 1.0 is free and has no in-app purchases. If paid features are added in the future, they will be protected by the parental check and this policy will be updated first.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data with anyone, because we do not collect any.

Children

Bubble Words is designed for young children. Because it collects no personal information, it does not collect information from children. It is built to follow Google Play's Families Policy and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
