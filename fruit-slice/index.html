Fruit Slice – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy covers the Android game Fruit Slice (package com.omicse.fruitslice), published by omidcc ("we").

What we collect

We do not collect your name, email address or any account details, and we run no servers of our own. The game has no accounts of its own. It shows ads and measures how the game is used through Google services, described below, which process some data on our behalf.

What stays on your device

Your progress (scores, level, stars, blades, dojos, achievements, statistics and settings) is saved only on your device. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

Google Play Games

The game offers leaderboards through Google Play Games Services. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game sends your scores to Google so they can appear on the leaderboards, where other players can see them next to your Play Games gamer name. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy. We never receive your Google account details, name or email address.

You can play without signing in; leaderboards are then simply unavailable. You can manage or delete your Play Games data, including scores, in the Google Play Games settings of your Google account.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: optional "rewarded" ads you can choose to watch for in-game stars, and occasional full-screen ads between games. To show and measure ads, the AdMob SDK collects your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. Google uses this data under the How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services page and the Google Privacy Policy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to improve it, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records game events, such as starting and finishing a run, scores, level-ups, purchases with in-game stars and ads watched, together with an app instance ID, device and app information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how long and how often the game is used. This data is processed by Google and shown to us only in aggregated reports; it does not include your name or email address. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The game uses the internet connection (for ads, analytics and leaderboards), vibration for touch feedback, and the advertising ID permission used by the ads and analytics services. It does not access your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data leaves your device only for the Google services described above: Google Play Games (scores), Google AdMob (ads) and Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics).

Deleting your data

Progress stored on your device is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. Google keeps detailed analytics data for no more than 14 months and then deletes it automatically. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device, write to the address below.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and its ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
