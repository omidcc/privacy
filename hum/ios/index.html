Hum for iOS – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy covers the Hum app for iPhone and iPad (bundle ID com.omi.musicplayer), published by omidcc ("we"). The Android app has its own policy at tunerapps.net/privacy/hum.

What we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information. Hum has no account of its own and runs no servers of our own. It plays the audio files already on your iPhone or iPad, in your Apple Music library, or in folders you choose.

What stays on your device

Hum reads your Apple Music library, the folders you link from the Files app (including iCloud Drive and Google Drive), and the files you import into Hum, including song files, embedded album art, and folder cover images, to build your Library, Home and Search screens. Imported files are copied into Hum's own storage on your device. It never uploads this library, your file names, or your listening history anywhere. Your playlists, favorites, play history and preferences (shuffle, repeat, equalizer settings, sleep timer) are stored in a local database on your device only, and are removed when you delete the app.

Lyrics lookup

When you look up or sync lyrics for a song, Hum sends that song's title, artist and duration to lrclib.net, a free, open lyrics database, to find a match. No other file or personal data is sent. This only happens when you open a song's Lyrics screen or the lyrics editor.

Advertising

Hum shows ads through Google AdMob to support free use of the app: a small banner on a few screens, and an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch for a temporary ad-free period. Before any ad is requested, Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your consent if you're in the EEA, UK or another region that requires it. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, and, to the extent you've consented, to personalize them — handled under the Google Privacy Policy. You can view or reset your ad personalization choices any time in Google Ads Settings.

Permissions

Hum asks for access to your media library (Apple Music) to build your library. From the second launch it also asks for permission to track across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency) before the advertising identifier is used; if you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads are not personalized. Folders and files from the Files app are only opened when you pick them. Hum does not access your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Backups

Your playlists and settings may be included in your iCloud or device backup. That backup is managed by Apple, not by us, and isn't accessible to us.

Purchases

Hum Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data with anyone. The only data that leaves your device is the lyrics-lookup request described above, and the advertising data described above.

Children

Hum isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
